Transcript

Welcome to the Study Design Sandbox. Here is the situation: one county, four hundred similar patients, and one mobile screening and coaching program meant to lower A1C. The numbers are made up, so we know the truth. The program really lowers A1C by six tenths of a point, but patients also tend to improve by about half a point over six months on their own. Your job is to pick an evaluation design and see how close it gets to the true effect of minus six tenths. Six buttons let you switch between post-test only, before-and-after, controlled before-and-after, randomized controlled trial, factorial, and Solomon four-group. For each design, read the bars, the apparent program effect, and the box called What can go wrong. When you are done exploring, jot down which design you would choose for your own project, and create your reflection on the tool. You can pause any audio on this page and resume where you left off.
