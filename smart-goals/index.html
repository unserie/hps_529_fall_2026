How it holds up

    Indicator alignment

      This coach looks for patterns in your wording. It cannot judge meaning, and a clean check does not guarantee a grade. Achievable and Relevant are yours to judge, so the coach asks questions instead of scoring them.

      See a worked example

      Revise and check again to unlock a worked example on a different topic, so you learn the pattern without copying it. It shows all three levels of objective.

      Output objectiveBy March 2027, trained staff will deliver 8 falls-prevention classes to at least 120 adults aged 60+ in [Town].
      Outcome objective (initial)By June 2027, at least 120 adults aged 60+ in [Town] who attended the classes will report a 20% increase in confidence doing balance exercises.
      Impact objective (long-term)By December 2031, the number of fall-related injuries among adults aged 60+ in [Town] will fall by 15%.
      Indicator for the outcome objectivePercent change in mean balance-confidence score (pre vs. post survey) among class attendees aged 60+ in [Town], June 2027.
