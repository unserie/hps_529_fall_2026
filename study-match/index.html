Transcript

Welcome to Study Design Match. Before you choose an evaluation design for your own project, this activity is a warm-up. You will see six short scenarios, and each one describes how a program was evaluated. Sort each scenario into the design it describes, either by dragging it onto a design, or by selecting it and then choosing a design. As you read, ask yourself four things. Was there a baseline measurement before the intervention? Was there a comparison group, and if so, was it chosen at random? Is anyone testing the effect of the pre-test itself, which points to the Solomon four-group design? And is more than one intervention tested alone and in combination, which points to a factorial design? Use the key for the symbols. If you miss a scenario, you get a nudge first, and on a second miss the answer is shown so you can learn from it. Mistakes here are free, so take your time. You can pause any audio on this page and resume where you left off.

Transcript

Nice work. Look back at which designs you hesitated on, or needed a nudge or the answer for. Those are the ones to review before you write up the design for your own project in Week 4. A good habit is to check the same four things every time: was there a baseline, was there a comparison group, was assignment random, and is anything extra being tested, such as a pre-test or more than one intervention. When you are ready, press New round for a fresh set of scenarios, then create your reflection on the tool. After that, try the Study Design Sandbox to see how the same program can look very different depending on the design you choose.
