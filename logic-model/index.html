9. Assumptions

CDC guide

Beliefs about how the program will work and what it needs, drawn from research, experience, and common sense. Example: funding will stay secure, or gaining knowledge will lead to a change in behavior.

    10. Contextual factors

    CDC guide

    Conditions in the surrounding environment that influence your program and that you have little control over, such as other agencies' initiatives, socioeconomic conditions, social norms, and politics.
